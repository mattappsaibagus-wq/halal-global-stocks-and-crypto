Data sources & how current they are

  • Crypto prices: LIVE. Coinbase Exchange, fetched by your browser every 15 s. Each price shows how many seconds ago it last traded.
  • US stock prices: live with a free Finnhub key (below), refreshed every 60 s during US market hours. Without a key: official close.
  • Indonesia, Malaysia, Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Turkey: official closing price of the date shown (Yahoo Finance). There is no free, reliable real-time feed for these exchanges; the scan runs right after each market closes.
  • Shariah ratios: Yahoo Finance company data, as last reported by the company (quarterly).
  • News: Google News, which collects headlines from publishers. Every item shows its publisher, age and a link to the original. Headlines are information only, never a signal.
  • A red STALE tag means the latest price is more than 3 trading days old (suspension, delisting or a feed problem). Don't trade on it.

Which individual signals carry an edge?

Live track record (signals scored against real prices)

  • Edge = the signal's average 20-day return minus the same stocks' average 20-day return on any day. Near zero means the signal is noise.
  • Expectancy = average result of each BUY traded with its stop and target, in R (1R = the amount risked), after a 0.2% round-trip cost. Positive over many trades is the goal.
  • Entry is the next day's open, so no future price is used. News and fundamentals are not replayed. Consecutive-day signals overlap, so N is signal-days, not independent trades.
  • Past results do not guarantee future results. This is research tooling, not financial advice.
